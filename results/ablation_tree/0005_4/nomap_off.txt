## IWTU Reengineering Project Assessment

- The meeting reviewed DOE EM’s management of the IWTU reengineering project.
- GAO assessed the project’s cost estimate, schedule estimate, and earned value management (EVM) data against best practices.
- The project was reported to be in phase two as of March 2019, with phase one focused on identifying problems and phase two focused on implementing corrections.
- By February 2019, spending on the first two phases was about $150 million, exceeding the original estimate by about $64 million.
- The project was more than one year behind schedule.
- Future phases were expected to require additional shutdowns, adjustments, and testing.

## Cost and Schedule Estimation

- GAO found the cost estimate only partially met best practices.
- The estimate was not built using a standard work breakdown structure.
- It did not include full life-cycle costs because later phases had not yet been fully estimated.
- Assumptions and risk explanations in the cost estimate were insufficient.
- GAO found the schedule estimate partially met best practices.
- The schedule had logical sequencing overall, but contained long-duration activities and some unreasonable positive and negative float.
- Several activities showed negative float, indicating schedule delays.
- GAO noted that weak cost and schedule estimates limit management’s ability to plan resources and assess risk.

## EVM Data and Management Use

- EM had an EVM system and used it for decision-making.
- GAO found the EVM system was sufficiently comprehensive and useful for management purposes.
- GAO found the reliability of the EVM data only partially met best practices.
- The data contained missing and negative values, and these anomalies were not fully explained in monthly reporting.
- GAO concluded that unreliable EVM data could weaken management oversight.

## DOE Operational Release Oversight

- DOE established an operational release milestone in 2016 for projects with startup risk.
- EM submitted an operational release plan and provided multiple progress briefings to PMRC.
- EM did not fully meet the requirement for quarterly reporting.
- PMRC had recommended clarifying authorization responsibilities before startup.

## SBW Disposal Challenges

- Sodium-bearing waste (SBW) was treated as mixed high-level waste because it contained both radioactive and hazardous chemical components.
- Disposal of SBW was constrained by federal law, EPA/RCRA requirements, and state-authorized procedures.
- EM preferred disposal at WIPP, but three major barriers remained:
  - WIPP permit restrictions
  - Federal legal restrictions on high-level waste disposal at WIPP
  - Limited WIPP disposal capacity
- DOE was seeking a new interpretation of the legal definition of high-level waste.
- DOE had not provided a clear timeline for policy or regulatory changes.
- GAO noted that applying the new interpretation to SBW could lead to long-term litigation.

## Calcine Waste Disposal Challenges

- EM had previously selected hot isostatic pressing as a treatment approach for calcine waste.
- Development of that approach was later paused because the technology was not mature, costs were high, and regulatory uncertainty remained.
- EM was evaluating bin set removal/retrieval testing and possible direct disposal options.
- No final disposal pathway or timeline had been established for calcine waste.

## GAO Conclusions and Recommendations

- GAO concluded that EM had made progress in some technical and oversight areas, but major gaps remained in cost estimation, scheduling, EVM data reliability, and waste disposal planning.
- GAO made five recommendations:
  - Improve cost estimating practices.
  - Improve schedule estimating practices.
  - Ensure EVM data reliability.
  - Develop a disposal strategy and timeline for SBW.
  - Develop a treatment/disposal strategy and timeline for calcine waste.

## Action Items

- DOE EM: Improve IWTU reengineering project cost estimates and align them with best practices.
- DOE EM: Improve IWTU reengineering project schedule estimates and align them with best practices.
- DOE EM: Ensure the reliability of EVM data used for IWTU project oversight.
- DOE EM: Develop a strategy and timeline for SBW disposal.
- DOE EM: Develop a strategy and timeline for calcine waste treatment and disposal.
