## Scope and Review Method

- The meeting focused on GAO’s review of DOE/EM’s IWTU reconstruction project.
- The review covered cost estimates, schedule estimates, earned value management (EVM), independent reviews, and the disposal challenges for SBW and calcine waste.
- GAO reviewed DOE/EM documents, policies, and contractor reports, and interviewed relevant federal and state officials.

## IWTU Cost Estimate Assessment

- GAO evaluated the cost estimate primarily for completeness.
- At the time of review, EM had developed cost estimates only for the first two phases of the reconstruction project.
- GAO noted that incomplete cost estimates limit the ability to assess accuracy, documentation, and credibility.

## IWTU Schedule Assessment

- GAO evaluated the project schedule primarily for whether it was well constructed.
- EM had not yet developed a complete schedule for the full reconstruction effort because the work was being carried out in phases.
- GAO noted issues affecting schedule quality, including missing logical links and limited ability to show overall critical path impacts.

## EVM Review

- GAO reviewed EVM data for the period from March 2017 to February 2018.
- EM’s EVM system showed strengths in system existence and management use.
- GAO found that EVM data reliability was only partially met because of missing values, negative values, and insufficient explanation of anomalies.
- Unreliable EVM data can reduce the quality of management decision-making.

## Independent Reviews and PMRC Oversight

- GAO reviewed DOE/EM policies and PMRC review records.
- PMRC was intended to provide independent review for projects entering the operational release phase.
- GAO noted that DOE/EM had taken some steps, but quarterly reporting requirements were not fully followed.

## SBW Disposal Challenges

- EM wanted to send treated sodium-bearing waste (SBW) to WIPP.
- Three main barriers were identified:
  - WIPP permit restrictions on certain wastes previously managed as HLW
  - Federal law prohibiting HLW disposal at WIPP
  - Limited WIPP capacity
- DOE was exploring a new interpretation of the HLW definition, but no complete strategy or timeline was identified.

## Calcine Waste Treatment and Disposal Challenges

- DOE previously selected hot isostatic pressing as the treatment method for calcine waste.
- Development of that option was paused due to concerns about technology maturity, cost, risk, and uncertainty in the disposal path.
- The termination of the Yucca Mountain repository plan undermined the original disposal assumption.
- DOE was continuing research into alternatives and retrieval/pilot activities, but no complete strategy or timeline was identified.

## GAO Conclusions

- GAO concluded that DOE/EM only partially met best practices for cost estimation and schedule development.
- GAO found that the EVM system was used by management, but the underlying data were not reliable enough.
- GAO also found that DOE/EM lacked clear long-term strategies for both SBW and calcine waste.

## Action Items

- DOE/EM: Develop a cost estimate for the IWTU/SBW effort that aligns with best practices.
- DOE/EM: Develop a schedule estimate for the IWTU/SBW effort that aligns with best practices.
- DOE/EM: Improve the reliability of EVM data used for IWTU oversight.
- DOE/EM: Develop a final disposal strategy and timeline for SBW.
- DOE/EM: Develop a treatment and disposal strategy and timeline for calcine waste to support the 2035 goal.
