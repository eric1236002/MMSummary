## Background and Regulatory Context

- The transcript discusses the regulation and commercialization of cell-cultured meat.
- The technology is still in the research and prototype stage, and commercial production methods, final product composition, and production costs remain uncertain.
- The report frames innovation as a process from invention to production and then sales.

## FDA and USDA Regulatory Roles

- FDA is responsible for food safety for most domestic and imported foods, except products under USDA jurisdiction.
- USDA is responsible for meat, poultry, processed egg products, and catfish.
- FDA generally oversees cell-cultured meat up to the “point of harvest.”
- USDA oversees processing, packaging, and labeling after harvest.
- For cell-cultured seafood other than catfish, FDA is responsible, but this was not formally recorded in a public document, creating some confusion.

## Cell-Cultured Meat Production Process and Open Questions

- The general production process includes biopsy, cell banking, growth in bioreactors, harvest, and food processing.
- Key unresolved issues include:
  - animal source and sampling frequency
  - whether genetic engineering is used
  - whether antibiotics are used
  - composition of the culture medium
  - whether scaffolds are edible or non-edible
  - how the point of harvest is defined and regulated
  - scalability and cost reduction
  - final product composition
  - environmental, animal welfare, and health impacts
  - consumer acceptance
  - product naming and labeling

## FDA and USDA Collaboration Mechanisms

- FDA and USDA have created joint public meetings, an interagency agreement, and three working groups.
- The three working groups cover premarket assessment, labeling, and jurisdiction transfer.
- The report states that these collaboration mechanisms only partially reflect GAO’s seven best practices for interagency collaboration.
- The seven best practices referenced include defining outcomes, assigning leadership and roles, involving relevant participants, identifying resources, maintaining written guidance, tracking progress, and coordinating across organizational cultures.

## GAO Findings and Recommendations

- GAO found that the agencies had not fully incorporated collaboration best practices into their agreement and working groups.
- GAO recommended that FDA and USDA more fully integrate these practices.
- GAO also recommended that the responsible regulator for cell-cultured seafood other than catfish be clearly identified and publicly documented.

## Agency Responses

- FDA and USDA agreed with most of GAO’s recommendations.
- The agencies stated that the current agreement is a high-level framework and should not include excessive detail.
- GAO emphasized that clearer collaboration mechanisms and written documentation are important for this emerging technology.

## Action Items

- FDA: Further clarify and document the regulatory responsibility for cell-cultured seafood other than catfish.
- USDA: Further clarify and document the regulatory responsibility for cell-cultured seafood other than catfish.
- FDA and USDA: Incorporate interagency collaboration best practices more fully into the agreement and working groups.
