## Regulatory Overview

- The meeting discussed a GAO report on the regulation and commercialization progress of cell-cultured meat in the United States.
- Cell-cultured meat is still in a mid-development stage, and the production technology, process, and final product composition have not been fully determined.
- The report outlined five main stages from innovation to commercialization: biopsy, cell banking, growth, harvest, and food processing.

## FDA and USDA Roles

- FDA is responsible for most food safety oversight and regulates the early stages of cell-cultured meat production up to the harvest point.
- USDA is responsible for meat, poultry, processed egg products, and catfish, and takes over regulation after harvest, including processing and labeling.
- FDA and USDA coordinate through joint meetings, mutual agreements, and working groups.

## Unresolved Regulatory and Technical Issues

- Open questions remain about tissue sample collection source and frequency.
- It is not fully resolved whether genetic engineering is used.
- The use of antibiotics remains an open issue.
- The composition of the growth medium is still under discussion.
- The use of scaffolds remains unresolved.
- The definition of the harvest point is still unclear.
- Challenges remain in scaling up production and reducing costs.
- Safety, product composition, environmental impact, animal welfare impact, labeling, and consumer acceptance remain key issues.

## GAO Findings and Gaps

- GAO found that FDA and USDA have established collaboration mechanisms but have not fully aligned with cross-agency collaboration best practices.
- For cell-cultured seafood other than catfish, the regulatory responsibility has not been formally documented, although both agencies verbally indicated that FDA is responsible.

## GAO Recommendations

- GAO made six recommendations in total: three to FDA and three to USDA.
- The recommendations focus on better incorporation of collaboration best practices.
- The recommendations also call for clearer goals and evaluation mechanisms within working groups.
- GAO recommended formally documenting which agency regulates cell-cultured seafood other than catfish.

## Action Items

- FDA: Strengthen collaboration practices and formally document oversight responsibilities for cell-cultured seafood other than catfish.
- USDA: Strengthen collaboration practices and improve working group goals and evaluation mechanisms.
- FDA and USDA: Clarify regulatory boundaries and reduce overlap in oversight of cell-cultured meat products.
