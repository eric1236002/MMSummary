## CCDF Program Overview and Reporting Requirements

- The CCDBG Act and CCDF regulations govern state child-care programs for low-income working families.
- States submit 3-year State Plans to OCC to request CCDF funding and describe program administration.
- States are required to report CCDF expenditures and the number of children served by subsidies.
- The fiscal years 2019–2021 Plan Preprint included new regulatory requirements and an added request for states to report results of program-integrity and fraud-fighting activities.

## OCC Oversight and Monitoring

- OCC and state lead agencies share responsibility for overseeing and protecting the integrity of the CCDF program.
- OCC reviews and approves State Plans through its Program Operations Division and regional program staff.
- OCC launched a formal Monitoring System in fiscal year 2019 to review selected states over the 3-year State Plan period.
- The Monitoring System is intended to ensure compliance, identify technical-assistance needs, and identify promising practices.
- The Monitoring System covers 11 topic areas, including program integrity and accountability.

## Internal Controls and State Plan Review Process

- OCC has not established finalized written policies to guide staff review and approval of State Plans.
- OCC used training materials, draft documents, and a summary protocol, but these were not formal policies for continuous use.
- OCC stated that flexibility is needed due to staffing changes and regional differences.
- The lack of written policies limits consistency in State Plan review and retention of organizational knowledge.

## Program-Integrity Results Reporting

- OCC requested states to report results of program-integrity activities, but most approved State Plans did not include this information.
- In a review of 51 approved State Plans, 43 plans did not report results of program-integrity activities as requested.
- OCC has not defined what it considers to be the “results” of program-integrity activities.
- OCC also did not communicate clear internal criteria or external guidance to states about this information request.
- OCC officials said the information was requested on an informational basis and was not required for State Plan approval.

## Improper Payment Reporting and Corrective Action

- CCDF is designated as a high-priority program under improper-payment requirements.
- States estimate and report improper payments on a 3-year rotational cycle, and HHS reports aggregate results.
- The FY 2019 CCDF gross improper payment estimate was approximately $325 million, with an estimated rate of 4.53 percent.
- OCC oversees the improper-payment reporting process by approving preliminary documents, case sample changes, Joint Case Reviews, and final reports.
- States with error rates at or above 10 percent must submit Corrective Action Plans and updates if the error rate remains high.

## Fraud Risk and Internal Control Standards

- Federal standards require managers to consider fraud risk when identifying, analyzing, and responding to risks.
- GAO’s Fraud Risk Framework and OMB guidance emphasize antifraud control activities and risk-based fraud prevention.
- The transcript notes that fraud risk can exist even if fraud has not yet occurred.
- OCC has not completed a fraud risk assessment for CCDF.

## Action Items

- OCC: Finalize written policies for the State Plan review and approval process.
- OCC: Define what information is needed regarding the results of program-integrity activities.
- OCC: Communicate program-integrity results reporting requirements to states.
- OCC: Communicate internal criteria for reviewing program-integrity results to staff.
- OCC: Develop written standards for reviewing Corrective Action Plans.
- OCC: Finalize written policies for post-submission follow-up on Corrective Action Plans.
- OCC: Develop criteria for evaluating whether state program-integrity activities are effective.
- OCC: Evaluate whether the Self-Assessment Instrument and Fraud Toolkit can be used to collect monitoring information.
- ACF: Complete a fraud risk assessment for the CCDF program.
