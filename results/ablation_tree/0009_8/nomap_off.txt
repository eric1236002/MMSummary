## Program Integrity Oversight and CAP Review

- OCC/ACF lacked clear written policies for overseeing CCDF program integrity.
- Criteria for CAP review, follow-up tracking, and monitoring were not clearly defined.
- Information needs for oversight and review processes were not clearly specified.

## Impact of Current Gaps

- There was no assurance that state corrective actions addressed the root causes of improper payments.
- There was no assurance that program-integrity controls were effective.
- Available tools were not fully used to gather information for monitoring fraud risk.
- The CCDF fraud risk assessment / antifraud strategy had not yet been completed.

## GAO Recommendations and HHS Response

- GAO made 9 recommendations, including:
  - Requiring OCC to establish written policies.
  - Defining information requirements.
  - Creating standards for CAP review and follow-up.
  - Establishing criteria to assess program-integrity effectiveness.
  - Evaluating the feasibility of using available tools to collect monitoring data.
  - Requiring ACF to complete the fraud risk assessment.
- HHS agreed with all 9 recommendations.

## Action Items

- OCC: Develop written policies for CCDF program-integrity oversight.
- OCC: Define information needs for oversight, CAP review, and monitoring.
- OCC: Establish standards for CAP review, follow-up tracking, and program-integrity effectiveness assessment.
- OCC: Evaluate the feasibility of using available tools to collect information for fraud monitoring.
- ACF: Complete the CCDF fraud risk assessment / antifraud strategy.
